Statistics Calculator

By Hafiz Muhammad Usman · Last updated 1 November 2026 · From mean and median to skewness and confidence intervals

Paste or type a list of numbers, pick the statistic you want (or choose All Statistics), and read the results. It is quick for homework, lab reports and checking a spreadsheet.

Population and sample versions of variance and standard deviation are listed separately so you can use the one your question asks for.

What it can do

  • Centre and spread: count, sum, mean, median, mode, range, minimum and maximum, midrange.
  • Variation: population and sample standard deviation and variance, coefficient of variation, mean and median absolute deviation, standard error of the mean.
  • Position: quartiles and IQR, 10th and 90th percentiles, and an outlier count using the 1.5 × IQR rule.
  • Shape and other means: skewness, excess kurtosis, geometric mean, harmonic mean and root mean square.
  • Inference: a 95% confidence interval for the mean.

How to use it

  1. Enter your numbers separated by commas, for example 4, 8, 15, 16, 23, 42.
  2. Choose a single statistic or All Statistics.
  3. Press calculate and read each result.
  4. For two variables or hypothesis tests, use the dedicated tools linked below.

Worked check with 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9

The mean is 40 ÷ 8 = 5. The squared deviations from the mean add up to 32. Population variance is 32 ÷ 8 = 4, so the population standard deviation is 2. Sample variance is 32 ÷ 7 ≈ 4.571, so the sample standard deviation is about 2.138. The median is (4 + 5) ÷ 2 = 4.5, the mode is 4 and the range is 7.

Tips for better results

  • Use population formulas when you have the whole group, and sample formulas when your data is a sample of a larger group.
  • Check for typing mistakes. One extra digit can change the mean a lot.

Frequently asked questions

Sample or population: which do I choose?

If the data is every member of the group, use population. If it is a sample used to estimate a larger group, use sample (n − 1).

Can it do regression or ANOVA?

Yes, with the correlation and regression, ANOVA and hypothesis test calculators.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The calculations run in your browser.
