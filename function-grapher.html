Function Grapher

By Hafiz Muhammad Usman · Last updated 1 November 2026 · From y = x² to 3D surfaces and box plots

The function grapher guides you in three steps: choose the kind of graph, enter the functions, then explore the plot. It covers ordinary function graphs as well as the less common types that textbooks ask for, and it also draws statistics charts from your data.

Because it runs in the browser, there is nothing to install, and it works on a phone as well as a laptop.

Open the Function Grapher →

What it can do

  • Functions and curves: function graph, unit circle, 3D surface, polar graph, parametric curves, inequalities, implicit curves (conics), slope fields for dy/dx, and contour plots.
  • Statistics and data: bar chart, scatter plot, line chart, area chart, pie chart, histogram, box plot, normal distribution, binomial distribution and linear regression.
  • Maths tools: the complex plane (Argand diagram) and vector addition.
  • Explore a function: compare it with a second function g(x), plot its derivative f′(x), shade the area between a and b, or open a table of values.
  • Quick starts: common functions such as x², sin(x), eˣ, ln(x), |x| and √x can be added with one tap.

How to use it

  1. Open the grapher and choose a graph type, for example Function Graph.
  2. Type your function, or tap one of the quick buttons such as x² or sin(x).
  3. Press to plot, then drag the graph to move around it.
  4. Add g(x), the derivative, an area or a table of values to see more.

Graph y = x² − 4x + 3

Factorising gives (x − 1)(x − 3), so the parabola crosses the x-axis at x = 1 and x = 3. The vertex is at x = 2, where y = 4 − 8 + 3 = −1, so the lowest point is (2, −1). It crosses the y-axis at y = 3. Plot it and check that the picture matches these four numbers.

Tips for better results

  • Use brackets for fractions, for example 1/(x+2), so the whole denominator is used.
  • If a graph looks empty, drag the view around. Your function may be outside the current window.
  • For trigonometric graphs, check whether the angle mode is degrees or radians.

Frequently asked questions

Can I plot 3D graphs?

Yes. Choose 3D Surface to plot a function of x and y.

Can it graph data, not just functions?

Yes. Bar, scatter, line, area, pie, histogram, box plot and regression charts take your own numbers.

Does it work offline?

Yes, after your first visit.
