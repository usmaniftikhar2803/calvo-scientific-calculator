Rules for fractions

  • Add / subtract: a/b + c/d = (a×d + c×b) ÷ (b×d). Then simplify.
  • Multiply: a/b × c/d = (a×c) ÷ (b×d).
  • Divide: a/b ÷ c/d = a/b × d/c (flip the second fraction).
  • Simplify: divide the top and bottom by their HCF. Find it with the LCM and HCF calculator.

Example

1/2 + 1/3 = (1×3 + 1×2) ÷ (2×3) = 5/6, which is already in simplest form and equals 0.8333 as a decimal. And 3/4 ÷ 3/8 = 3/4 × 8/3 = 24/12 = 2.

A mixed number shows the whole part and the leftover fraction: 7/3 = 2 1/3.

What a fraction means

A fraction shows part of a whole. In 3/4 the numerator (3) says how many parts you have and the denominator (4) says how many equal parts the whole is cut into. A proper fraction such as 3/4 is smaller than 1. An improper fraction such as 7/3 is larger than 1. A mixed number such as 2 1/3 shows the whole part and the leftover fraction together.

Using this calculator

Type the numerator and denominator of the first fraction, choose +, −, × or ÷, then type the second fraction and press Calculate. The result shows each step, the answer in simplest form, the mixed number when the answer is bigger than 1, and the decimal value. The boxes accept whole numbers only. To enter a negative fraction, put the minus sign on the numerator, for example −3 / 4. To enter a mixed number, convert it to an improper fraction first (see below).

Worked examples

Add: 2/3 + 3/4. The common denominator is 12, so 8/12 + 9/12 = 17/12 = 1 5/12 = 1.416667.

Subtract: 5/6 − 1/4 = 10/12 − 3/12 = 7/12 = 0.583333.

Multiply: 2/5 × 15/8 = 30/40. Divide top and bottom by 10 to get 3/4.

Divide: 3/5 ÷ 9/10 = 3/5 × 10/9 = 30/45 = 2/3.

The calculator uses cross multiplication, so its steps may show a larger denominator such as 24 instead of 12. The final answer is always reduced to the same simplest form.

Mixed numbers and improper fractions

To change a mixed number to an improper fraction, multiply the whole part by the denominator and add the numerator. For 3 2/5 this is (3 × 5 + 2) / 5 = 17/5. To go back, divide the numerator by the denominator: 17 ÷ 5 = 3 remainder 2, so 17/5 = 3 2/5.

Fractions, decimals and percentages

To turn a fraction into a decimal, divide the top by the bottom: 3/8 = 0.375. Multiply by 100 for the percentage, so 3/8 = 37.5%. To go the other way, write the decimal over a power of 10 and simplify: 0.75 = 75/100 = 3/4.

Equivalent fractions and comparing

Multiplying the top and bottom by the same number gives an equivalent fraction, so 2/3 = 4/6 = 6/9. To compare two fractions, cross multiply. For 3/4 and 5/7, 3 × 7 = 21 and 5 × 4 = 20. Since 21 is bigger, 3/4 is the larger fraction.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the denominators. 1/2 + 1/3 is 5/6, not 2/5.
  • Dividing without flipping. Flip only the second fraction, then multiply.
  • Forgetting to simplify the final answer.
  • Mishandling negative signs. −3/4, 3/−4 and −(3/4) are the same value, but −3/−4 is positive.

FAQ

How do I add fractions with different denominators?

Cross multiply: a/b + c/d = (a×d + c×b) ÷ (b×d), then simplify by dividing by the HCF.

How do I divide fractions?

Keep the first fraction, flip the second one and multiply.

How do I simplify a fraction?

Divide the numerator and denominator by their highest common factor.

Can the denominator be zero?

No. A fraction with denominator 0 is undefined.

How do I enter a mixed number like 2 1/3?

Convert it first: (2 × 3 + 1) / 3 = 7/3. Then type 7 and 3 in the boxes.

Can I type decimals in the boxes?

No, only whole numbers. Convert the decimal to a fraction first, for example 0.5 = 1/2.

Why does the calculator show a different denominator in the steps?

The steps use cross multiplication, which gives a common denominator that may not be the smallest. The final line is divided by the HCF, so it is in simplest form.

What does it show when the answer is a whole number?

It shows just the number. For example 3/4 ÷ 3/8 shows 2.
