How the difference is found

Total days are the exact number of calendar days between the two dates. The years, months and days breakdown subtracts year by year, month by month and day by day, borrowing the length of the previous month when needed. Working days count Monday to Friday and ignore public holidays.

Worked example

From 1 January 2026 to 1 October 2026 is 273 days: that is 39 weeks exactly, or 9 months. Counting both the first and last day (include end date) gives 274 days.

Useful for

Semester length, assignment deadlines, project timelines, countdowns to exams, and finding the date 90 days after an admission or result date.

FAQ

How many days are between two dates?

Pick both dates and press the button. Calvo shows the exact days, weeks, and the years-months-days breakdown.

Is the end date included?

Not by default. Tick include end date to count both the first and the last day.

What are working days?

Monday to Friday between the two dates. Public holidays are not removed.

How do I find the date after 90 days?

Use the second tool: enter the start date and 90. Use a negative number to go back in time.

Does it handle leap years?

Yes, leap years and different month lengths are counted exactly.
