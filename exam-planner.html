Common questions

How do I make my exam datesheet here?

Type the subject, pick the date and add the time and room if you know them. Tap Add exam. The list sorts itself by date and the countdown to your next paper starts at once.

What does the countdown show?

If you entered a time it counts days, hours, minutes and seconds to the start of the paper. If you left the time empty it counts the calendar days left, and shows Today on the exam day.

What are free days between exams?

Each exam shows how many full days you get after the previous paper. It helps you decide how to share your revision time between subjects.

Can I add the datesheet to my phone calendar?

Yes. Tap Add to calendar to download a file that opens in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and Outlook. Every exam also gets a reminder one day before.

Can I print my datesheet?

Yes. Tap Print. The page prints only the title and the list of exams, without the forms and buttons.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser on this device. Nothing is sent to a server. If you clear the site data of your browser the exams are removed, so use Copy or the calendar file as a backup.

Does the alarm ring when the page is closed?

No. A website can only play sound while its page is open, so keep the tab open and the screen on. For a reminder that works when the page is closed, tap Add to calendar and your phone calendar will notify you one day before each paper.
