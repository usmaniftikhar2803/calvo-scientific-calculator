Formulas

Worked example

(3 + 4i)(1 − 2i) = (3·1 − 4·(−2)) + (3·(−2) + 4·1)i = 11 − 2i. The modulus of 3 + 4i is √(9 + 16) = 5.

FAQ

How do I multiply complex numbers?

Use (a + bi)(c + di) = (ac - bd) + (ad + bc)i, because i squared is -1.

How do I divide complex numbers?

Multiply the top and bottom by the conjugate of the denominator, then divide by c squared plus d squared.

What is the modulus of a complex number?

The distance from the origin: square root of a squared plus b squared.

What angle does Calvo give?

The principal argument between -180 and 180 degrees, also shown in radians.

Does the square root show both roots?

Yes. A non-zero complex number has two square roots, the principal one and its negative.
