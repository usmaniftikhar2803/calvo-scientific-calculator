Polymerization (concept)the process of joining many small monomer molecules together to form a large polymer chain

FormulaExpression
Enthalpy ChangeΔH = H_products − H_reactants

Enthalpy of ReactionΔH_rxn = ΔH_products − ΔH_reactants
Enthalpy of Formation (concept)the heat change when 1 mole of a compound forms from its elements in their standard states
Enthalpy of Combustion (concept)the heat released when 1 mole of a substance burns completely in oxygen

Exothermic vs Endothermic (concept)exothermic reactions release heat (ΔH negative); endothermic reactions absorb heat (ΔH positive)
Specific Heat Capacity (concept)the amount of heat needed to raise the temperature of 1 g of a substance by 1°C
