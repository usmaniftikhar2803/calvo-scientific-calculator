The aggregate formula

Most merit lists use a weighted percentage: Aggregate = Σ (Obtained ÷ Total × Weightage), where the weightages add up to 100.

Worked example

Matric 900 of 1100 at 10%: 900 ÷ 1100 × 10 = 8.18. Intermediate 800 of 1100 at 40%: 800 ÷ 1100 × 40 = 29.09. Entry test 120 of 200 at 50%: 120 ÷ 200 × 50 = 30.00. Aggregate = 8.18 + 29.09 + 30.00 = 67.27%.

Important

Weightages, and even which marks count, change by university, program and year. Some add interview or hafiz-e-Quran marks. Always follow the official admission notice. This calculator is only for estimating. To turn a percentage into a grade, see the percentage calculator.

FAQ

How do I calculate my merit aggregate?

Multiply each part's percentage (obtained ÷ total) by its weightage and add them up. Weightages should total 100.

Which weightage should I use?

Use the one printed in your university's admission notice or prospectus. They differ between universities and programs.

Can I add more rows, like an interview?

Yes. Press Add row and enter its marks and weightage.

Is the result my official merit?

No. It is an estimate. The university's published merit list is final.
