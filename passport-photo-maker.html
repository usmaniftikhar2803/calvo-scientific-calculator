How it works

Choose a photo and Calvo does the hard work for you. An AI model that runs inside your browser finds you in the picture and removes the background. A second model finds your face, so the photo can be cropped and centred in the usual passport style, with the head in the middle and a little space above it. The result appears at once, on the background colour you pick.

The AI can also straighten a tilted or sideways photo, make a dark face a little lighter and clean small spots on the skin, and you choose the background colour before you start. Nothing is final until you say so. You can change the colour, switch to another size, zoom, move or straighten the photo, make it brighter, and clean the edges with the Erase and Restore brush. When you are happy, download the single photo or make a print sheet with several copies.

How to take a good photo

  • Stand or sit straight and look into the camera with a neutral face. Keep your mouth closed.
  • Use soft light from the front, for example near a window. Avoid shadows on one side of your face.
  • Show your full face, your head and the top of your shoulders. Hair should not cover your eyes.
  • Ask someone to take the photo at eye level. Photos taken from very close or from below look stretched.
  • Remove anything that hides your face or reflects light, such as glare on glasses.

Printing your photos

Open the Print sheet tab, choose your paper and how many copies you want, and download the PDF. When you print it, choose 100% or Actual size. If the printer shrinks the page to fit, your photos will come out smaller than planned. Print on photo paper for the best colour, and cut along the thin lines with a ruler and a sharp cutter.

FAQ

How do I make a passport size photo from a photo on my phone?

Tap Choose your photo and pick a picture where your face looks straight at the camera. Calvo removes the background, puts your face in the middle of a 35 x 45 mm frame and shows the result. Change the background colour or position if you like, then tap Download photo.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. The AI runs inside your browser, so your photo never leaves your phone or computer. The first time you use it, your browser downloads about 12 MB of AI files, which are then kept for next time.

What background colour should I choose for a passport photo?

White is the safest choice and is what most forms accept. Some forms ask for light blue, blue or red, so always read the instructions on your own form. You can switch colours in one tap and compare.

What size is a passport photo?

A common size is 35 x 45 mm, which is 413 x 531 pixels at 300 DPI. Other sizes exist, for example 2 x 2 inch for some visas, or 25 x 35 mm. The Size tab has the common ones and a custom option.

How do I print 6 or 8 passport photos on one A4 page?

Open the Print sheet tab, choose A4 and the number of copies, then tap Download PDF. Print the PDF at 100% (Actual size) and do not choose Fit to page, or the photos will print too small.

How can I make my photo smaller than 20 KB, 35 KB or 50 KB?

Before you tap Download photo, choose your limit under File size limit. Calvo finds the best quality that stays under it. You can also use the Photo Resizer tool for more size options.

The AI cut off some hair or left some of the old background. What can I do?

Open the Fix edges tab. Choose Erase and rub over any leftover background, or choose Restore and rub over any part of you that was removed by mistake. Tick Check edges on green to see mistakes more clearly.

Why does the page say Quick mode?

Very old phones or browsers cannot run the AI. In Quick mode Calvo treats the colour around the edges of your photo as the background, which works best in front of a plain wall. You can still clean it up with Fix edges.

Will this photo be accepted for my passport or CNIC?

Official offices set their own rules about photo size, background, expression, glasses and print quality, and they can change. This tool helps you prepare a photo at the usual size, but always check the rules of the office or form you are applying to.

Can the AI straighten a tilted photo and make a dark face lighter?

Yes. Before you choose your photo you can tick three options. The AI can straighten a tilted or sideways photo, make your face lighter if it is too dark, and remove small spots and marks on your face. Afterwards you can change the amount in the Face tab, or turn each one off.
