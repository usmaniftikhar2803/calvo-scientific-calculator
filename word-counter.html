How the counts work

Words are groups of characters separated by spaces or line breaks. Sentences end with a full stop, question mark, exclamation mark or the Urdu full stop. Paragraphs are blocks of text separated by blank lines. Reading time assumes 200 words per minute for silent reading, and speaking time assumes 130 words per minute.

Useful for

Assignment and essay limits, scholarship and personal statements, social media posts, and checking how long a speech or presentation will take.

Keyword density

The keyword list shows the words you use most (ignoring very short and common words) so you can spot repetition in your writing.

FAQ

How do I count words in my text?

Paste your text in the box. The word count updates as you type.

How is reading time calculated?

Words divided by 200, the typical silent reading speed of an adult. Speaking time uses 130 words per minute.

Does it work for Urdu text?

Yes. Words are counted by spaces, and the Urdu full stop and question mark are recognised for sentences.

Are spaces counted in characters?

Both counts are shown: characters with spaces and characters without spaces.

Is my text saved or sent anywhere?

No. Everything is counted inside your browser.
