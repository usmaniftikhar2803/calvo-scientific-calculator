Notepad

A simple notepad. Tap + to write, it saves as you type, and works offline.

Saves automatically on this device and works offline. Clearing browser data erases notes.

How it works

Tap the + button to start a note. Type a title and your text, and it saves automatically. Tap Notes to go back to the list, and use the search box to find an old note.

What the notepad is good for

This notepad is built for quick, private text. Students use it for lecture points, a list of formulas to revise, assignment deadlines, a plan for the week, or a draft of an email before it is sent. Because it opens instantly and needs no sign-up, it is useful when you just want to write something down without setting up an account.

Titles, search and organising

The first line of a note becomes its title in the list, so start each note with a clear first line such as "Physics Ch 3 - Motion" or "Assignments - Week 5". The list shows the most recently edited notes with a short preview, and the search box looks through both titles and the full text of every note. A consistent naming habit (subject first, then topic) makes search much faster once you have many notes.

Making a to-do list

There is no special checklist mode, but plain text works well. Write one task per line and start each with a dash. When a task is done, put a tick or the word DONE at the start of the line, or move the line to the bottom of the note. Short lists that you rewrite every day are easier to keep up than long ones.

Share and back up your notes

Notes are stored only in this browser on this device. They are not synced to other phones or computers. If a note matters, use the Share button. On most phones it opens the share menu so you can send the note to WhatsApp, email or another app. Where sharing is not available, the text is copied so you can paste it anywhere. Sending an important note to yourself is a simple way to keep a backup.

Privacy and limits

  • Your notes are not uploaded, so Calvo cannot read them and cannot recover them for you.
  • Clearing browser data, removing the app or switching to a different browser makes the notes disappear on that device.
  • Private or incognito windows may delete notes when you close them.
  • A deleted note cannot be restored, which is why the app asks you to confirm first.
  • Browsers give each site a few megabytes of storage. That is a lot of plain text, but this is not the place for photos or large files.

Tips for using it offline

Open the page once while you have internet. After that it keeps working without a connection, including search and editing. Notes save every time you type, and again when you leave the page or switch to another app, so you do not need to press a save button.

FAQ

Does the notepad work offline?

Yes. After you open it once, it works without internet. Your notes are saved on your device, not on a server.

Where are my notes saved?

In your browser on this device. They stay after you close the page or restart the phone, but are removed if you clear the site data of the browser or the app.

Do notes save automatically?

Yes. Notes save as you type.

How do I delete a note?

Open the note and tap Delete at the top right.

Do my notes sync between my phone and my laptop?

No. Each browser keeps its own notes. Use the Share button to move a note from one device to another.

Is there a limit on how long a note can be?

There is no fixed page limit. The practical limit is the storage that your browser gives the site, which is large for plain text.

Can I recover a note I deleted?

No. Once a note is deleted it is gone from this device, so share or copy anything you may need later.

Can someone else read my notes?

Notes are stored in your browser, not on a server. Anyone who can unlock and use your phone or browser can open them, so avoid keeping passwords or banking details here.
