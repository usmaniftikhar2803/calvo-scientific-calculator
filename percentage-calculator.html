Percentage formulas

  • X% of Y = (X ÷ 100) × Y
  • X is what % of Y = (X ÷ Y) × 100
  • Percentage change = ((New − Old) ÷ Old) × 100
  • Increase by P% = Number × (1 + P/100); decrease by P% = Number × (1 − P/100)

Examples

Exam marks: you scored 412 out of 550. Percentage = 412 ÷ 550 × 100 = 74.91%.
Discount: a Rs. 2,500 item with 20% off costs 2500 × 0.8 = Rs. 2,000.
Growth: from 80 to 100 is a change of (20 ÷ 80) × 100 = 25% increase.

FAQ

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Divide the percentage by 100 and multiply by the number. For example, 15% of 200 = 0.15 × 200 = 30.

How do I find the percentage of marks?

Divide obtained marks by total marks and multiply by 100.

What is the difference between percentage change and percentage points?

Going from 10% to 15% is a change of 5 percentage points, but a 50% relative increase.

Why does a 20% increase followed by a 20% decrease not return to the start?

Because the decrease applies to the larger new number. 100 → 120 → 96.
