Citation style

APA: type titles in sentence case (only the first word and names in capitals). MLA and Harvard: use the capitals of the original title.

Common questions

How do I make a citation with this generator?

Choose a style (APA, MLA or Harvard) and a source type, then fill in the author, title, date and link. The reference and the in-text citation appear as you type. Tap Copy to paste it into your assignment.

Which citation styles are supported?

APA 7th edition, MLA 9th edition and Harvard (Cite Them Right format). You can switch style at any time and your saved list is rewritten in the new style.

How do I enter the authors?

Write one author per line, as First Last or Last, First. Tick the organisation box if the author is a company, university or government body.

Can I make a full reference list?

Yes. Tap Add to list after each source. The list is sorted alphabetically, as every style requires. Copy it or download it as a Word file with hanging indents.

Will my sources be saved?

They are saved in your browser on this device so you can come back later. Nothing is sent to a server. Clearing the browser data removes them.

Should I check the result before submitting?

Yes. The tool follows the standard rules, but your university may use a small variation. Check capital letters in titles and compare with your department guide.
