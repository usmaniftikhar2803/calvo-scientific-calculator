Marks to Percentage Calculator

Add your subjects with obtained and total marks. Your overall percentage updates as you type, and your entries are remembered on this device.

0.00%

Formula

Percentage = (Total obtained marks ÷ Total marks) × 100. Marks of all subjects are added first, then divided by the combined total.

Example

Mathematics 50/100, Computer 69/100, Urdu 71/100: obtained = 190, total = 300, percentage = 190 ÷ 300 × 100 = 63.33%.
