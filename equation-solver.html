Equation Solver

By Hafiz Muhammad Usman · Last updated 1 November 2026 · Step-by-step, works fully offline

The equation solver shows how an answer is reached, not just the answer. Pick the type of equation, enter the coefficients, and the solution is worked out line by line so you can compare it with your own method.

It does not use AI or a network connection, so results are produced by fixed algebra rules and work offline.

Open the Equation Solver →

What it can do

  • Polynomials: linear, quadratic, cubic, quartic and biquadratic equations.
  • Systems: two-variable and three-variable systems of linear equations.
  • Other families: exponential, logarithmic, absolute value, radical, rational, trigonometric (sin, cos, tan), cube root and reciprocal forms, plus proportion and direct or inverse variation.
  • Step-by-step output: each solution is shown with the method, such as the discriminant for a quadratic.

How to use it

  1. Choose the equation type from the list.
  2. Enter the coefficients in the boxes. The form shows the general shape, for example ax² + bx + c = 0.
  3. Press solve and read the steps from top to bottom.
  4. Substitute the answer back into the original equation to confirm it.

Two worked examples

Quadratic: 2x² − 5x − 3 = 0. The discriminant is b² − 4ac = 25 + 24 = 49, so x = (5 ± 7) ÷ 4, giving x = 3 and x = −1/2.

System: 2x + y = 7 and x − y = 2. Adding the two equations gives 3x = 9, so x = 3, and then y = 1. Check: 2(3) + 1 = 7 and 3 − 1 = 2.

Tips for better results

  • Make sure the equation is in standard form (everything on one side) before you read off the coefficients.
  • A negative discriminant means no real roots for a quadratic. The solver will tell you.
  • Always substitute your answer back to catch sign errors.

Frequently asked questions

Does this use AI?

No. It uses algebra rules in your browser, which is why it works offline and gives consistent results.

Can it solve my specific word problem?

Turn the word problem into an equation first, then pick the matching type. For free-form questions use the AI solver.

Is there a separate quadratic page?

Yes, the quadratic equation solver focuses on that one type.
