How it works

Add your photos with the camera or from the gallery. Each page is straightened and cropped to the paper, then cleaned with the filter you choose. You can adjust the crop, rotate, change the order and apply brightness and contrast. When you are happy, Create PDF builds one PDF that you can download or share. All the work happens on your device.

Useful for

Scanning handwritten assignments, notes and forms, both sides of an ID card, fee challans and certificates, and sending them as a single small PDF for admission or scholarship portals, email or WhatsApp.

FAQ

How do I scan a document with my phone?

Tap Camera and take a photo of the page, or tap Gallery to pick photos you already have. The page edges are found automatically. Check the crop, choose a filter such as Magic Color or Black & White, add more pages if you need to, then tap Create PDF.

Does it find the edges of the page automatically?

Yes. It looks for the page against the surface behind it and flattens it into a straight rectangle. It works best on a plain, darker surface in good light. If it misses, tap Crop and drag the four corners yourself.

What do the filters do?

Original keeps the photo as it is. Magic Color evens out the light and makes the paper white. Lighten brightens dark photos. Grayscale gives a soft grey scan, and B&W gives the sharpest black text and the smallest file. You can fine-tune brightness and contrast, and apply one filter to every page.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. Everything, including the edge detection and the PDF, is done inside your browser, so your photos never leave your phone or computer.

How can I make the PDF small enough for an upload portal?

Open PDF settings and type a maximum size such as 500 KB or 2 MB. Calvo lowers the quality step by step until the PDF fits, or tells you if it cannot get that small. B&W pages are the smallest.

Can I scan both sides of my ID card on one page?

Yes. In PDF settings, set Layout to ID card. Scan the front and then the back, and both are placed on one A4 page at real card size.

Can I reorder, rotate or delete pages?

Yes. Use Move left and Move right to change the order, Rotate to turn a page, and Delete to remove it. Tap a thumbnail to jump to that page.

Why was my HEIC photo skipped?

Some browsers cannot open HEIC files from iPhones. Change the camera format to Most Compatible, or save the photo as JPG, and add it again.
