Image to PDF Converter

Turn photos of assignments, forms and documents into a single PDF. Set a maximum size if a portal limits your upload.

Image to PDF

Add images and tap Convert to PDF.
Your photos stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

How it works

Each photo is resized to a sensible size, saved as a compressed JPEG and placed on its own PDF page. You choose A4, Letter, or a page that fits the image exactly, and a margin if you want white space around the photo. Everything happens on your device, so it also works for private documents.

Useful for

Submitting handwritten assignments, joining the pages of a form, uploading documents to admission and scholarship portals, and sending several photos as a single file on WhatsApp or email.

FAQ

How do I convert photos to a PDF?

Tap the box and choose your photos, put them in the right order with the arrow buttons, pick a page size and tap Convert to PDF. Then download the file or share it.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF is made inside your browser, so your photos never leave your phone or computer.

How can I make the PDF small enough for an upload portal?

Type a maximum size such as 500 KB or 2 MB before converting. Calvo lowers the image quality step by step until the PDF fits, or tells you if it cannot get that small.

What is the best way to scan handwritten assignment pages?

Take clear photos in good daylight with the page flat and fully inside the frame. Add them in page order, rotate any sideways photo with the rotate button, and use A4 with a small margin.

Can I rotate or reorder pages?

Yes. Each image has buttons to move it up or down, rotate it by 90 degrees, or remove it before you convert.

Why was my HEIC photo skipped?

Some browsers cannot open HEIC files from iPhones. Change the camera format to Most Compatible, or save the photo as JPG, and add it again.
