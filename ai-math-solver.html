AI Math Solver

By Hafiz Muhammad Usman · Last updated 1 November 2026 · Type, snap a photo or speak

The AI Math Solver explains a problem in plain language. You can type the question, upload a photo of a textbook problem, or use your voice, and choose between a Quick Answer and a Detailed Explanation with the steps.

It runs on Google Gemini using your own free API key from Google AI Studio. The key is saved only in your browser and your question is sent from your browser to Google, not through a Calvo account, so there is nothing to sign up for on Calvo.

What it can do

  • Three ways to ask: type, add a photo (with crop and rotate so only the question is sent), or dictate with the microphone.
  • Two levels of answer: a short quick answer when you only want to check your result, or a detailed step-by-step explanation when you want to learn the method.
  • Explain simply: ask for the same solution in simpler words, or listen to it read aloud.
  • Urdu explanation: a button explains the solution in Urdu.
  • Saved questions: keep the questions you want to revisit and clear them whenever you like.

How to use it

  1. Get a free key from Google AI Studio (this is Google's own page) and paste it into the solver once.
  2. Type your question, or add a photo or voice input.
  3. Choose Quick Answer or Detailed Explanation and press Solve.
  4. Read the steps, then try the next problem yourself. Use Explain Simply or Urdu if a step is unclear.

A good way to use it: check, don't copy

Solve 2x² − 5x − 3 = 0 on paper first. Then ask the solver. The discriminant is 25 + 24 = 49, so x = (5 ± 7) ÷ 4, which gives x = 3 or x = −1/2. If your working differs, compare it line by line with the explanation. You can also confirm the roots with the equation solver, which works without the AI.

Tips for better results

  • AI can make mistakes. Check important answers with the equation solver, the calculators on Calvo, or your teacher.
  • Crop a photo to a single question for better results.
  • Google sets the limits on free keys, so very heavy use may hit its usage cap.

Frequently asked questions

Is the AI solver free?

Calvo does not charge for it. It uses your own free Gemini key, and Google's free-tier limits apply.

Where is my key stored?

Only in your browser's local storage on your device. Clear your browser data to remove it.

Can it read a photo of my homework?

Yes, using Gemini's image understanding. Crop to the question for best accuracy.

Is it reliable?

It is a study helper, not an authority. Verify answers before you rely on them.
