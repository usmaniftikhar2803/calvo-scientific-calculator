About these 9th & 10th Class Computer Science formulas

This is a compact page, because computer science at this level relies on a handful of methods: converting between binary, decimal, octal and hexadecimal, the rules of Boolean algebra, and the behaviour of the logic gates, plus some units for IT fundamentals. Most of these are procedures rather than formulas, so the best way to use the page is to follow the steps on paper with your own numbers, and then check your answer by converting back.

How to revise 9th & 10th Class Computer Science

  • Learn the powers of two up to 2¹⁰ (1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024). Binary conversions become fast once you know them.
  • Memorise the truth tables of AND, OR, NOT and XOR. Every Boolean expression is built from these four.
  • Always check a conversion by going back. If decimal 45 becomes binary 101101, then 101101 must give 45 again.
  • For hexadecimal, group the binary digits in fours from the right. Each group is one hex digit.

Worked example

Question. Convert the decimal number 45 to binary, and check it by converting back.

Divide by 2 and keep the remainders: 45 → 22 r1, 22 → 11 r0, 11 → 5 r1, 5 → 2 r1, 2 → 1 r0, 1 → 0 r1.

Read the remainders from bottom to top: 101101.

Check: 1×32 + 0×16 + 1×8 + 1×4 + 0×2 + 1×1 = 45.

Common mistakes

  • Reading the remainders from top to bottom. They must be read from the bottom up.
  • Mixing up OR and XOR. XOR gives 1 only when the two inputs are different, while OR also gives 1 when both are 1.
  • Starting the position count at 1 instead of 0 when converting binary to decimal. The rightmost digit is 2⁰.

FAQ

How do I convert a decimal number to binary?

Divide the number by 2 again and again, writing down each remainder, until you reach 0. Then read the remainders from the last to the first.

What is the output of an XOR gate?

The output is 1 only when the two inputs are different. If both inputs are the same, 0 or 1, the output is 0.

How many addressable bytes do n address lines give?

They give 2ⁿ bytes. With 10 address lines that is 1,024 bytes, which is 1 KB.
