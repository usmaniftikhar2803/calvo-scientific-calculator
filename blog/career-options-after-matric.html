Career Options After Matric

August 2026 · 6 min read

Matric results open up several genuinely different paths, and the right choice depends more on your interests and strengths than on which option "sounds" most prestigious. Here's an honest overview of the main routes.

1. FSc Pre-Medical

The path toward MBBS, BDS, Pharm-D, and allied health sciences (Physical Therapy, Nursing, Medical Lab Technology). Best suited if you're genuinely interested in biology and want to keep medical-related options open — it's a demanding two years regardless of whether you ultimately pursue medicine.

2. FSc Pre-Engineering

The path toward engineering disciplines, computer science, architecture, and other math-heavy degree programs. A strong fit if you enjoy physics and math more than biology, and want to keep both engineering and computer science options open.

3. ICS (Intermediate in Computer Science)

Focuses on computer science, mathematics, and physics without the broader FSc science load. A good option if you already know you want to pursue Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related IT field, and prefer a curriculum more directly aligned with that goal.

4. I.Com (Intermediate in Commerce)

Covers accounting, business, and economics, leading toward BBA, BS Accounting & Finance, or ACCA/CA professional qualifications. A strong choice if you're more interested in business, finance, or entrepreneurship than science or engineering.

5. Diploma of Associate Engineering (DAE) / Vocational Training

A more technical, hands-on 3-year path (through technical boards) that leads directly into skilled technical roles or, with a bridging exam, into second-year engineering university programs. Often overlooked, but it's a practical route for students who prefer applied, hands-on learning over exam-heavy theory.

How to Choose

  • Pick based on which subjects you're actually strong in and enjoy — not based on what your friends or family expect, since you're the one who has to get through the workload.
  • If you're unsure between Pre-Medical and Pre-Engineering, honestly compare how you feel about Biology versus Physics/extra Math — that's usually the clearest signal.
  • Keep in mind that most paths have some flexibility later (deficiency courses, equivalency exams) — an early choice narrows options but rarely closes them completely.
