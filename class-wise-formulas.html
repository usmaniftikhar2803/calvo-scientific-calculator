About these pages

Each page lists formulas chapter by chapter, with a search box and a print option. Formulas come from the Calvo formula library, and many key formulas have a short Urdu explanation (اردو وضاحت). 1st and 2nd Year chapter placement follows the usual Punjab and Federal board outline; Chemistry, Biology and Computer Science are given as one FSc page covering both years.

FAQ

Which classes are covered?

9th and 10th Class, and FSc 1st and 2nd Year (Physics and Math by year; Chemistry, Biology and Computer Science for both years together).

Are the pages free and printable?

Yes. Every page has a Print / Save as PDF button.

Can I find a formula quickly?

Yes. Each page has a search box.
