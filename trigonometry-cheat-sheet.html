Unit circle

Each point is (cos θ, sin θ). The x-coordinate is cosine and the y-coordinate is sine.

FAQ

How do I remember the unit circle?

Remember the first quadrant values for 30, 45 and 60 degrees, then use symmetry: the other quadrants have the same values with different signs (ASTC: All, Sine, Tangent, Cosine).

What is sin squared plus cos squared?

It always equals 1. This is the Pythagorean identity and the other two are found by dividing it by cos squared or sin squared.

How do I convert degrees to radians?

Multiply by pi and divide by 180. For example 60 degrees is pi/3.

Where are tan 90 and cot 0 values?

They are undefined because the denominator (cos 90 or sin 0) is zero.
