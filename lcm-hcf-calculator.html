What are HCF and LCM?

HCF (Highest Common Factor, also called GCD) is the largest number that divides all the given numbers exactly. LCM (Least Common Multiple) is the smallest number that all of them divide into exactly.

Methods

  • Prime factorization: write each number as a product of primes. HCF takes the lowest power of each common prime; LCM takes the highest power of every prime.
  • Euclid's algorithm: replace the larger number by the remainder of dividing it by the smaller, until the remainder is 0. The last divisor is the HCF.
  • Link between them: for two numbers, HCF × LCM = the product of the numbers.

Example

For 12 and 18: 12 = 2² × 3 and 18 = 2 × 3². HCF = 2 × 3 = 6. LCM = 2² × 3² = 36. Check: 6 × 36 = 216 = 12 × 18.

Prime factorisation: a worked comparison

Take 84 and 90. Factorising gives 84 = 2² × 3 × 7 and 90 = 2 × 3² × 5. For the HCF, take each common prime with its smallest power: 2 × 3 = 6. For the LCM, take every prime that appears with its largest power: 2² × 3² × 5 × 7 = 1,260.

Check with the key relationship for two numbers: HCF × LCM = a × b. Here 6 × 1,260 = 7,560 = 84 × 90. This identity does not hold for three or more numbers, so do not use it there.

Euclid's algorithm for large numbers

When the numbers are too big to factorise by hand, divide repeatedly and keep the remainder. For 252 and 105: 252 = 2 × 105 + 42; 105 = 2 × 42 + 21; 42 = 2 × 21 + 0. The last non-zero remainder, 21, is the HCF. Then LCM = (252 × 105) ÷ 21 = 1,260.

Adding fractions

The LCM of the denominators is the least common denominator. For 5/12 + 7/18, the LCM of 12 and 18 is 36, so the sum is 15/36 + 14/36 = 29/36. The HCF is used to simplify: 18/24 simplifies by dividing both parts by their HCF 6 to give 3/4.

Try these first, then check the answers

1. Find the HCF and LCM of 36 and 48.

36 = 2² × 3² and 48 = 2⁴ × 3. HCF = 2² × 3 = 12, LCM = 2⁴ × 3² = 144. Check: 12 × 144 = 1,728 = 36 × 48.

2. Three traffic lights change every 40, 60 and 90 seconds. When do they change together again?

LCM(40, 60, 90) = 360 seconds, so after 6 minutes.

3. Find the greatest number that divides 59 and 83 leaving remainder 3 each time.

Subtract the remainder first: 56 and 80. HCF(56, 80) = 8, and 8 is larger than the remainder 3, so it is valid.

FAQ

How do I find the HCF of two numbers?

Use Euclid's algorithm: divide the larger by the smaller, then repeat with the remainder until it is 0. The last non-zero remainder is the HCF.

How do I find the LCM?

For two numbers, LCM = (a × b) ÷ HCF. For more numbers, find the LCM of two, then with the next, and so on.

Is HCF the same as GCD?

Yes. HCF, GCD (greatest common divisor) and GCF (greatest common factor) mean the same thing.

Can I use decimals or negatives?

No. Use positive whole numbers only.
