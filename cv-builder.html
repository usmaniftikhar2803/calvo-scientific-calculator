How it works

Pick a design (Classic, Modern with a sidebar, or Minimal), choose a colour and fill in the form. The CV is built on your device as a clean A4 PDF with real text and proper page breaks if it runs to a second page. Sections you leave empty do not appear.

Tips for a good CV

Keep it to one or two pages, start each point with an action word, add numbers where you can (for example, improved speed by 30%), and list your most recent education or job first. Always check spelling before you send it.

FAQ

How do I make a CV with this builder?

Choose a design and colour, then fill in your details: personal information, a short summary, education, experience, projects and skills. The live preview updates as you type. When you are happy, tap Download PDF.

Is it suitable for fresh graduates with no experience?

Yes. Keep Education first, add your projects, skills, certifications and achievements, and leave Experience empty. Empty sections are left out automatically. You can use Fill an example to see how a fresher CV looks.

Is the PDF text selectable and job-portal friendly?

Yes. The PDF contains real text, not a picture, so it can be copied and read by online application systems. Use English letters, as other scripts are not supported in the PDF.

Can I add my photo?

Yes. Tap Add photo and choose a clear passport-style photo. It is cropped to a circle and shown on the CV. Photos are optional.

How do I fit my CV on one page?

Set Text size to Compact, shorten long points, and keep to your strongest 3 to 4 points per job or project. The preview shows how many pages you have.

Will my details be saved?

Your details stay in your browser on this device, so you can return and edit later. Use Clear everything to remove them. Nothing is sent to a server.

Can I change the order of my education and experience?

Yes. Use the Order setting to put Education or Experience first, and the arrow buttons on each entry to reorder entries.
