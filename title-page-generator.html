FAQ

How do I make a title page for my assignment?

Choose a design and colour, then fill in your university, title, course, name, roll number and teacher. The preview updates as you type. Tap Download PDF to save it, or Download JPG if you want an image.

Can I add the title page to my own assignment PDF?

Yes. Under the form, choose your assignment PDF. When you tap Download PDF, the title page is placed in front of your pages and you get one combined PDF.

Can I add my university logo?

Yes. Tap Add logo and choose an image. It is placed at the top of the page. Your logo is saved on your device so you can reuse it next time.

Will I have to type my details every time?

No. Your name, roll number, university and other details are saved on this device in your browser, so next time the form is already filled in. Use Clear my details to remove them.

Which size and format is the title page?

A4 portrait, as a PDF at print quality. You can also download it as a JPG image.

Can I use Urdu or other languages?

Yes. The title page is drawn exactly as you see it in the preview, so any language your device can display works. Names and titles that are very long are shortened with three dots so the page stays tidy.

Is my information uploaded to a server?

No. The title page is created inside your browser. Nothing you type or any logo you add is sent anywhere.
